Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Short version: inviting the bot to your server is the only thing you ever need to do. There's no account, no sign-up, no dashboard to log into. The bot doesn't store your messages, your username, or anything else, it reads a message, acts on it, and forgets it happened.

What the bot sees

To do its job, the bot has to read message content as it comes in, so it can check for Twitter/X, Instagram, TikTok, and Bluesky links. That check happens instantly in memory. Nothing is written to a database, a log file, or anywhere else. If a message doesn't contain one of those links, the bot doesn't do anything with it at all, it's not being recorded or analyzed in the background.

What happens when it finds a link

The bot deletes your original message and reposts the fixed-up version. To make that repost look like it came from you, it grabs your current Discord display name and avatar at that moment and uses them for the repost, it doesn't save a copy of either one anywhere. Next time you post, it just grabs them fresh again.

Tweet videos

For a tweet with video, the bot downloads the video and re-uploads it to Discord directly so it plays natively in your server, rather than only linking out to it. That video passes through the bot's memory just long enough to hand it to Discord; it's never written to disk or kept around afterward. If a video is too large to attach, the bot falls back to posting a plain link instead so Discord can still generate its own preview.

Third-party services

To build each tweet's card (text, translation, author, media), the bot queries api.fxtwitter.com. For Instagram, TikTok, and Bluesky, links are rewritten to use kkinstagram.com, tnktok.com, and fxbsky.app, so Discord can generate a proper preview directly from those sites once the link is posted. These are independent services I don't run or control, so it's worth knowing they exist, but I have no visibility into anything they do on their end.

What's never collected

No emails, no IP addresses, no server member lists, no analytics, no tracking. The bot doesn't ask for anything beyond what it needs at the moment a link shows up, and it doesn't hang on to any of it afterward.

Changes to this policy

If this ever changes, say if a future feature needs to store something, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change. As it stands today, nothing is being stored, full stop.

Questions

Reach out via the GitHub repo's issues page if you want to ask anything about this.
