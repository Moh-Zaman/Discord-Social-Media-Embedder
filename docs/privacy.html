Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

Short version: inviting the bot to your server is the only thing you ever need to do. There's no account, no sign-up, no dashboard to log into. The bot doesn't store your messages, your username, or anything else — it reads a message, acts on it, and forgets it happened.

What the bot sees

To do its job, the bot has to read message content as it comes in, so it can check for Twitter/X and Instagram links. That check happens instantly in memory. Nothing is written to a database, a log file, or anywhere else. If a message doesn't contain one of those links, the bot doesn't do anything with it at all — it's not being recorded or analyzed in the background.

What happens when it finds a link

The bot deletes your original message and reposts the fixed-up link. To make that repost look like it came from you, it grabs your current Discord display name and avatar at that moment and uses them for the repost — it doesn't save a copy of either one anywhere. Next time you post, it just grabs them fresh again.

Third-party services

The whole point of the bot is rewriting links to use fixupx.com (for Twitter/X) and kkinstagram.com (for Instagram) so Discord can generate a proper preview. Once that link is posted, Discord's own servers fetch the preview directly from those sites — the bot itself doesn't send them any of your data. Those are independent services I don't run or control, so it's worth knowing they exist, but I have no visibility into anything they do on their end.

What's never collected

No emails, no IP addresses, no server member lists, no analytics, no tracking. The bot doesn't ask for anything beyond what it needs at the moment a link shows up, and it doesn't hang on to any of it afterward.

Changes to this policy

If this ever changes — say, if a future feature needs to store something — this page will be updated and the date at the top will change. As it stands today, nothing is being stored, full stop.
