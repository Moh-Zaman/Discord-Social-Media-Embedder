Terms of Service

Last updated: September 13, 2026

Embed Bot is a small Discord bot that fixes up Twitter/X and Instagram links so they actually show previews in Discord. Here's the deal for using it.

Using the bot

You don't need to sign up for anything or create an account anywhere. Inviting the bot to your server is the only step required to use it. If you don't want it anymore, kick it from your server and that's it — nothing lingers.

What the bot does in your server

When it sees a Twitter/X or Instagram link, it deletes the original message and reposts the fixed-up version, styled to look like it came from whoever originally posted it. To do that, it needs a few permissions when you invite it: Manage Messages (to delete the original), Manage Webhooks (to repost as the original author), and access to message content (to actually spot the links). If you're not comfortable granting any of those, this bot isn't going to be a good fit for your server, and that's fine.

Acceptable use

Use the bot the way it's meant to be used. Don't try to abuse it, spam with it, or use it to get around Discord's own rules. You're still bound by Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines regardless of anything written here.
